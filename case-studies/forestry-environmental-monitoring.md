# Forestry and Environmental Monitoring

**Organisation:** Forestry & Citrus Research (FACIR) · **Context:** Co-founder and technology leadership work

## Problem space

Forestry and environmental research can involve distributed observations, field data collection and the need to turn operational measurements into usable information.

## Contribution

My work at FACIR involved technology direction and research initiatives across forestry, citrus and environmental technology. Areas included:

- Identifying relevant data sources and systematic data-collection approaches.
- Contributing to cloud-oriented solution architecture.
- Introducing software-development, DevOps and automated-testing practices.
- Exploring forestry monitoring, IoT and precision-forestry workflows.

## Technology themes

Python and software development, cloud architecture, DevOps, automated testing, IoT and geospatial/environmental data.

## What this demonstrates

This work connects software engineering and data workflows with an applied environmental domain. It also reflects experience working in a small research-oriented venture where technology choices need to support practical field and research needs.

*This case study describes the scope of work; it does not claim a deployed satellite-monitoring product or quantified environmental impact.*
