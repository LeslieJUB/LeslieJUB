# Inventory Data Engineering and Forecasting

**Context:** Operational data workflows at Capital Foods Pvt Ltd and backend data engineering work at BRIGE GmbH.

## Problem space

Inventory and replenishment decisions depend on timely, consistent information from sales, warehouse, supplier and stock systems. Fragmented data and slow reporting can make operational planning harder.

## Work described in my experience

- Designed ETL workflows integrating warehouse, sales and supplier information.
- Worked on database query optimisation for inventory tracking.
- Coordinated integration across ERP, warehouse-management and point-of-sale systems.
- Developed Kafka-based data-streaming workflows.
- Automated inventory forecasting and replenishment processes using Python.
- Worked with backend databases and data workflows supporting stock forecasting and demand prediction at BRIGE GmbH.

## Reported outcomes

My CV records the following outcomes from the relevant operational work: a 40% improvement in processing efficiency, query execution reduced from six seconds to two seconds, 50% fewer inventory-data discrepancies, and reductions in stockouts and overstocking through forecasting automation.

These are historical CV-reported outcomes, not independently audited benchmarks or results of this portfolio repository.

## Engineering concepts

ETL/ELT, data integration, SQL optimisation, event streaming, forecasting, operational reporting and data quality.
